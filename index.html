The problem

Every other AI compliance tool asks for your data first.

Trading floor chat, voice transcripts, internal email. This is the most sensitive data your firm holds, and most surveillance AI wants it sent to a shared cloud model before you can even run an evaluation. For a fund operating under market abuse rules, conduct regimes, or LP-mandated data residency clauses, that question goes well past procurement. It reaches the board, and it usually ends the conversation before it starts.

We built Ratioc the other way round. The model, the data, and the evidence all stay inside your perimeter, from the first message ingested to the final sealed pack.

How it works

Three steps, one perimeter.

01 — INGEST & PROTECT

Identities are protected first

Every message is pseudonymised the moment it arrives. Analysts work with anonymised conversations; only an approved reveal workflow can re-identify a person, and every reveal is logged.

02 — REASON

Reasoning grounded in your policies

A self-hosted language model checks each flagged conversation against your actual policy library. What comes back is a structured decision — the allegation, the reasoning, the policy it relied on, and a confidence score. Not a wall of prose.

03 — EVIDENCE

Sealed and ready for audit

When a case is closed, its findings, signals, and policy references are frozen into a signed evidence pack. It's ready for internal review, an audit, or a regulator request.

Deployment

Your hardware. Your model. Your evidence.

Ratioc runs on dedicated infrastructure inside your environment. There's no shared cloud tenant and no multi-tenant SaaS instance. The reasoning model is self-hosted, so nothing depends on a third-party AI provider and there's no path for internal communications to leave your network.
